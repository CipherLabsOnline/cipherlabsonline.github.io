Welcome To Cipher Labs

Learn And Instruct Cryptography

Cipher Labs offers a handful of cryptographic tools to teach students and researchers the fundamentals of cryptography.

Cipher Labs Desktop

Cipher Labs Desktop is the original elearning platform, that teaches cybersecurity students and researchers the fundamentals of modern cryptology and cryptanalysis. Use it to visually learn a wide range of cryptographic algorithms, and cryptanalysis methods. Supports both Windows and Linux

Cipher Labs Online

Cipher Labs Online is the web variant of Cipher Labs Desktop. Cipher Labs Online offers a handful of tools to experiment with a range of cryptographic functions and algorithms. Get started by clicking on "Workstation" in the top navigation, or the “Get Started” button below. Accessible via browser.

Lock Spot

Lock Spot was one of the very first tools that laid the foundation for Cipher Labs today. It is a cryptanalysis tool that uses machine learning to identify the cipher type and algorithm from just a short sample of ciphertext. Get started by clicking on "Lock Spot" in the top navigation, or the “Get Started” button below. Accessible via browser or through Cipher Labs Desktop application.

Resources

Explore our archive of Cipher Labs documentation and cryptography guides to master foundational concepts, understand how platform ciphers work, and build hands-on skills. Get started by clicking on "Resources" in the top navigation, or the “Get Started” button below. Accessible via browser or through Cipher Labs Desktop application.
