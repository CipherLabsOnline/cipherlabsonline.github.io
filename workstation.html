Classic Ciphers

Affine Cipher

Simple monoalphabetic substitution cipher that converts each letter to a number and applies a linear function.

Atbash Cipher

Simple monoalphabetic substitution cipher where each letter is replaced by its counterpart in the reversed alphabet.

Caesar/ROT13 Cipher

Popular monoalphabetic shifting cipher, by Julius Caesar.

Playfair Cipher

Popular digraph substitution cipher that encrypts plaintext into pairs of letters.

Polybius Cipher

A classic grid-based subsitution cipher that convert letters into pairs of numerical coordinates.

Porta Cipher

Popular polyalphabetic subsitution cipher, created by Giovanni Battista della Porta.

Rail Fence Cipher

A classic transposition cipher, that rearranges a message's letters into a zigzag pattern across multiple rows.

Vigenère Cipher

Popular polyalphabetic substitution cipher, created by Giovan Battista Bellaso.

Stegnographic Ciphers

Bacon Cipher

A 5-bit steganographic cipher by Sir Francis Bacon that hides letters using distinct text styles, binary, or 'A/B' variables.
